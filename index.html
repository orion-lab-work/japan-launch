AI Meeting Notes App

PRODUCTIVITY APP LOCALIZATION
BEFORE: Literal Translation
「AIを搭載した会議アシスタント。すべての会議を録音し、要約して整理します。」
Original: AI-powered meeting assistant.
AFTER: Japan Launch Native Copy
「AIが会議内容を自動で整理。
面倒な議事録作成や情報共有を、もっとスムーズに。」
Japanese Copy: AIが会議内容を自動で整理。議事録作成をもっとスムーズに。

Focus on Benefits, Not Features

The original copy emphasizes technical features. Japanese users often respond better to clear outcomes than feature lists. Instead of highlighting that the app is 'AI-powered', we focused on saving time.

Japanese User Tip & Insight

Japanese App Store users typically look for practical value first. Copy that answers 'How does this help me?' feels much more trustworthy than feature-heavy descriptions.

What Changed & Key Improvements

Replaced technical wording with user benefits
Improved natural Japanese flow
Adapted tone for Japanese readers
Focused on clarity instead of direct translation
